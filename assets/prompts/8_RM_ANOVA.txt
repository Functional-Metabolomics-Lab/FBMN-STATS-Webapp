**Page Summary: Repeated Measures ANOVA**

**Functionality:**
Repeated Measures ANOVA tests whether the means of three or more related (within-subject) groups differ significantly. It is the parametric counterpart to the Friedman test. The test is run per metabolite with pingouin's rm_anova. For each metabolite, Mauchly's test of sphericity is computed; when sphericity is violated, the Greenhouse-Geisser corrected p-value is reported, otherwise the uncorrected p-value.

**Requirements:**
- A within-subject factor column (condition/timepoint) with at least 3 levels.
- A subject identifier column that uniquely links rows across conditions.
- Each subject should have exactly one observation per condition (subjects with missing conditions are dropped by the test).

**Parameters:**
- **Within-subject factor:** Metadata column with the conditions/timepoints.
- **Subject column:** Metadata column identifying each subject.
- **Levels:** At least three levels of the within-subject factor to include.
- **P-value Correction:** The globally selected method for multiple comparison adjustment across metabolites (e.g., Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, Bonferroni, or none).

**Results:**
- *Feature Significance Plot:* Scatter plot of ln(F) vs. -log10(corrected p) for all features.
- *Results Table:* F-statistic, p-value, corrected p-value and boolean significance per feature.
- *Single Metabolite Boxplots:* Intensity distribution of a selected metabolite across the selected conditions.

Help the user select appropriate columns, interpret F-statistics and p-values, and understand multiple testing correction. If normality is doubtful, suggest the Friedman test as a non-parametric alternative.
